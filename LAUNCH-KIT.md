## Social launch post

I kept seeing the same startup advice: “Find a billion-dollar idea.”

But founders don't need another list of AI startup ideas. They need a better way to tell whether a problem is painful, whether someone will pay, and whether the economics make sense.

So I built **Million-Dollar SaaS Scout** — an open-source Agent Skill that helps research underserved markets, map competitors, score opportunities, model the customer math behind $1M ARR, and design validation experiments before writing code.

It is not a promise to make anyone a millionaire. It's a framework for making fewer, better startup bets.
