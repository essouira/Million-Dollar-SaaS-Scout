# Example requests

## Global discovery

Use the `saas-opportunity-scout` skill.

Find 10 underserved B2B SaaS or AI SaaS opportunities globally. Prioritize painful recurring workflows, identifiable buyers, existing spend and low-cost MVPs.

For each:
- Research the problem and current alternatives.
- Identify direct and indirect competitors.
- Provide sources and research dates.
- Separate facts, estimates and hypotheses.
- Propose a pricing hypothesis and calculate the customer count needed for $1M ARR.
- Score the opportunity and state evidence confidence.
- Identify the biggest unresolved risk.

Rank the 10 and recommend 3 customer-validation experiments. Do not build software.

## Vertical market investigation

Investigate [INDUSTRY] for [CUSTOMER SEGMENT] in [GEOGRAPHY].

Map the workflow, identify expensive or repetitive tasks, research incumbent software and substitutes, examine customer complaints and pricing signals, then propose differentiated opportunities.

Do not assume the market is underserved until you have investigated alternatives.

## Challenge my startup idea

Evaluate this idea as a skeptical investment committee:

[DESCRIBE IDEA]

Identify the buyer, problem severity, current alternatives, willingness-to-pay evidence, competitors, distribution strategy, unit economics and critical risks.

Score it using the repository's scoring model. Give a BUILD, VALIDATE, PIVOT or REJECT verdict. Explain what evidence could change your mind.

## $1M ARR feasibility

For this product: [DESCRIBE PRODUCT AND BUYER]

Build conservative, base and optimistic paths to $1M ARR. Show pricing, customer count, acquisition assumptions, gross margin, churn assumptions and key sensitivities.

Clearly label all hypothetical inputs. Do not present scenarios as forecasts.
